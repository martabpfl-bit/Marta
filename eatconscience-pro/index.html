How it connects

Two interfaces, one care relationship.

The app is the person’s everyday experience, and works on its own. When you’re connected, you set the clinical direction and the app carries it between consultations.

  1. 01

    Care Profile

    Objectives, needs, preferences, restrictions and current priorities, set and approved by you.

  2. 02

    Everyday behaviour & context

    Meal photos, short reflections and recipe choices your client decides to share. No exhaustive food logging.

  3. 03

    Useful insights

    AI organises what was shared and points out possible patterns, never scores or grades.

  4. 04

    Your professional decisions

    You review, judge and approve. Nothing clinical changes without you.

  5. 05

    A personalised app experience

    Your approved priorities shape recipes, prompts and the Companion in your client’s app.

Principles

Nutrition that adapts to real life.

  • Context, not compliance

    Minimal, useful context instead of exhaustive logging. Patterns to understand, never a grade.

  • No guilt by design

    No adherence scores, no punitive red states, no automatic criticism when someone eats outside a plan.

  • Calories aren’t the headline

    Calories and weight are never the default way the workspace is organised.

  • AI drafts, you decide

    AI organises information and drafts summaries or suggestions. Clinical judgement and approval stay with you.

Privacy & consent

Their app, their choice.

  • The person decides to connect with a nutritionist, and can disconnect.
  • You only see what they choose to share: meal photos, reflections, recipe preferences and messages.
  • Private Buddy conversations stay private unless the person explicitly shares them.
  • Clinical information is restricted to authorised professionals.
