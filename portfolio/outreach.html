    How I built it, and how I’d scale it.

    How I built it

    HeyReach + LinkedIn + Claude

    • Connected HeyReach to my LinkedIn account and to Claude.
    • Set up the messages I wanted to send, shaped by the information that came in for each person.
    • Claude turned that information into a meaningful, personal message instead of a template.
    • Tracked the conversations that followed: ~33% became meaningful, ~20 meetings in two months.

    How I’d scale it

    Same idea, built for a team

    • Lead lists from Sales Navigator, with no scraping tools: they break LinkedIn’s terms and put the account at risk.
    • Claude only uses facts from the profile, returns a fixed structure, and anything without a real detail goes to review instead of being sent.
    • I review the first messages by hand, then a sample every week.
    • Replies flow into the CRM by stage; Claude sorts them and suggests an answer, a person sends it.
    • Weekly numbers (accepted, replied, meaningful, meetings) decide what to change in the prompt.
    • Only business information, kept to the minimum, and anyone who says no is never contacted again.
