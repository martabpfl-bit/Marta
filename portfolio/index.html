01 · About

Hi, I’m Marta.

I work in Customer Success in tech and AI, with a background in customer service and account management.

At Nedzo, I became the first Customer Success hire and discovered the part of my work I enjoy most: being close to clients while also being close to the product. I loved understanding a client’s use case, helping turn it into something that actually worked, solving problems with the technical team and then staying with that client as they adopted and grew with the product.

That combination of people, technology and problem-solving is where I want to keep building my career.
