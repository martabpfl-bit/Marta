03 · Work

Things I’ve done.

Implementation · End to end

An AI agent for a small real estate team

Around 350 contacts a month on WhatsApp and by phone, answered by hand. I brought the client in, ran discovery, requirements, testing and training, and built it with a developer. When the original data source stopped giving access, I proposed an alternative route.

  • Stayed the client’s point of contact after go-live: weekly meeting and a shared Slack channel
  • Every issue written up the same way in Linear and Slack, with what I’d already tried
~50%of ~350 monthly contacts handled automatically
3 mo.pilot, and the client continued

Decisions · Stakeholders

Getting ten people to agree on scope

A construction company in the north of Portugal: about ten people, around 700 contacts a month, nothing shared or digitised, and everyone with a different idea of the process.

  • Listened, found the actual decision, brought in the right people
  • Proposed a workable path, confirmed it and documented it
  • Taught the client what had to happen first, and what was feasible
~700contacts a month, nothing digitised
Phase 1agreed and approved by the client

Proactive · Retention

Noticing a problem before the customer tells me

Nobody asked me to build it. I combined nine signals about each customer (usage, outcomes, appointments, automation performance, transcripts, sentiment, communication, lifecycle stage, renewal proximity) into one health status.

  • On an account that turned yellow: investigated, reached out, proposed solutions
  • Presented to leadership and approved for use
9signals, one status: green, yellow or red
Reneweda flagged customer subsequently renewed

07 · Beyond my CV

Who I am when I’m not working.

Nutrition was never really about food. It was about learning to sit with someone, ask the right questions and understand what they actually need before offering an answer. That habit never left me.

Since then, I have lived and worked across Portugal and Australia, travelled through Southeast Asia and kept choosing roles that put me close to people, problems and new things to learn.

That is still how I approach Customer Success. I care about the person behind the renewal number, because understanding what is blocking them is usually what improves the number.
